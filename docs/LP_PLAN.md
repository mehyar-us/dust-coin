# Mainnet LP-Seeding Plan (disclosure template)

**Status:** TEMPLATE — no mainnet LP exists. This document describes exactly what will be published *before* any mainnet liquidity pool opens. Nothing here has happened yet.

## The commitment

After the Great Sweep distribution completes, the founder seeds a small, **publicly disclosed** liquidity pool:

| Item | Planned value |
|---|---|
| DUST side | ~$25 worth of DUST (founder's personal purchase at market / from sweep remainder — disclosed which) |
| SOL side | ~$25 of SOL |
| Total seed | ~$50 |
| Venue | Raydium (or launchpad-graduated pool — disclosed at the time) |
| LP tokens | **Burned** (burn tx published) |

## Pre-open disclosure checklist (all published before trading opens)

- [ ] Exact DUST amount and SOL amount
- [ ] Source of the DUST (purchased vs. allocated — with tx signatures)
- [ ] Pool creation transaction signature
- [ ] LP token burn transaction signature
- [ ] Pool address

## What will NOT happen

- No hidden additional LP injections by the founder without same-day disclosure.
- No LP token retention by the founder (burn is the default; any deviation is announced *before* it happens with a reason).
- No "market making" by the founder's wallets. The ~$50 seed is the founder's total disclosed liquidity involvement.

## Why ~$50

This is a ~$100-budget launch (per the project's public economics). A thin pool is honest about what this is: a meme experiment, not a deep market. Thin liquidity means high slippage — stated here, not discovered by traders.
