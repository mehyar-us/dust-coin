# The Honesty Baseline

This document is part of the DUST project by design, not as legal boilerplate. The meme-coin market runs on manufactured hope; DUST runs on stated odds.

## Stated odds

- **$100 → $1M is lottery math.** "Your dust is a lottery ticket" is a description of the payoff *shape* (tiny entry, asymmetric upside) — not a prediction, not a plan, not a promise.
- **~95%+ of meme coins die.** Independent research (Solidus Labs, 2025–2026): ~98.6% of pump.fun tokens showed rug/pull-or-dump behavior; ~68.7% died on day one; ~1.5% ever graduated a bonding curve. Assume DUST follows the base rate until proven otherwise.
- **The most likely outcome is zero.** If you cannot afford to lose 100% of what you put in, do not participate.
- **Nothing in this project is financial advice.** Not the README, not the website, not the announcements, not the memes.

## What DUST is and isn't

| DUST is | DUST isn't |
|---|---|
| A meme with a fair-distribution event | An investment |
| An experiment in honest tokenomics | A promise of returns |
| 100% community-distributed (pro-rata, capped) | A team/insider allocation vehicle |
| Entertainment | Financial advice |

## No profit promises — ever

No page, post, or person speaking for DUST will promise returns, price targets, "moon" guarantees, or imply that buying DUST is a way to make money. Bounty/contest language, if any appears in phase 2, describes **contest rewards for contributions**, never earnings on investment. Anyone promising DUST profits is misrepresenting the project.

## Distribution is not a lottery

The Great Sweep distributes DUST **pro-rata to every eligible sweeper** — deterministic shares from a published formula, no random draws, no chance-based winners. "Lottery ticket" describes the *payoff asymmetry*, not the mechanism. This distinction is deliberate and legal, not just semantic.

## Risks, plainly

1. **Total loss** — the base case for meme coins.
2. **Copycats** — every viral coin spawns same-name fakes within hours. Verify the contract address from official channels only. There is no official $DUST on mainnet until a public, founder-signed announcement says so.
3. **Volatility** — if it trades, expect violent swings both directions.
4. **Smart-contract / operator risk** — the mechanics are public and simple by design, but code can have bugs. The devnet test log ([`tests/TEST_LOG.md`](../tests/TEST_LOG.md)) is public; mainnet deploys get the same treatment.
5. **Regulatory** — crypto rules vary by jurisdiction and change. DUST is a meme with no expectation of profit; participants are responsible for their own tax and legal compliance.

## The founder's commitments

- Zero team allocation. Zero insider wallets. No presale.
- Mint authority revoked, freeze authority revoked — verifiable on-chain.
- Post-sweep LP seed (~$50) with amounts and transaction signatures published *before* trading opens.
- No silent changes to the mechanics. The rules in [`SWEEP_MECHANICS.md`](SWEEP_MECHANICS.md) are the rules.
- If the project is abandoned, it will be announced as abandoned — no ghosting into a soft rug.
